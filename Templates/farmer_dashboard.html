        FARMING PRACTICES

        Agricultural Best Practices

        Improve soil health, crop productivity and farm sustainability with proven techniques.

        • Rotate crops to maintain soil fertility
        • Use integrated pest management
        • Monitor soil moisture and crop health
        Explore practices
        Getting started

        Alternate crop families between growing seasons, inspect plants weekly for early signs of pests, and use soil tests to guide fertilizer application.

        CROP INPUTS

        Fertilizers & Crop Protection

        Understand how to select and use agricultural inputs safely and effectively.

        • Choose nutrients based on soil testing
        • Follow approved product labels
        • Use protective equipment when handling chemicals
        View safety guidance
        Safe application

        Follow the exact instructions on the registered product label, including dosage, application timing, protective equipment and pre-harvest intervals. Consult an agricultural extension professional before using unfamiliar products.

        EXPERT SUPPORT

        Agricultural Extension Services

        Find the right kind of professional guidance for crops, pests and farm management.

        • Contact your local extension office
        • Ask about soil and crop diagnostics
        • Seek advice for disease or pest outbreaks
        How to get support
        Before contacting an adviser

        Prepare your farm location, crop type, planting date, photos of symptoms and details of recent treatments. Contact your state agricultural development programme or local extension office for available services.

        General educational guidance only. Always follow local regulations and qualified agricultural advice for chemical use.
